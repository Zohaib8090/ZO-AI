## AI-generated content policy (read this)

Play's AI-generated content policy expects apps that generate content with AI to prevent offensive output and to give people **an in-app way to report or flag offensive or harmful generated content**, and to use those reports to improve the safeguards. As of this branch the Android app has **no report/flag control** (it is a documented pre-launch gap, see `docs/PLAY-STORE.md` section "Before production"). Add one before the production release: a "Report this answer" action on each answer that sends the chat id, the message and an optional note to the operator (a new server op) or opens a pre-filled e-mail to the operator. Until then do not apply for production access.

Also state in the store listing (done: last paragraph of `full_description.txt`) that the app can make mistakes.
